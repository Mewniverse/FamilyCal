Tap a month to add it to your phone's calendar. Every event comes with an alert at 8:00 AM that day.

On Android?

Tapping a month downloads a file. Open it from your notifications or Files app, pick Google Calendar or Samsung Calendar, and confirm. Google Calendar may swap the 8 AM alert for its own default reminder, so check one event after importing. The Google link next to each event adds a single event the same way.

On iPhone?

Open this page in Safari. If you got the link inside Instagram, Facebook or another app, use that app's "Open in Safari" option first, or the calendar button may not work.

Want it to update by itself?

Subscribe once and new months show up automatically. On iPhone, turn off Remove Alerts on the subscribe screen to keep the 8 AM alerts. Google Calendar ignores them and uses its own reminders.
